Privacy

Underfoot does not require an account and the game itself does not include advertising, tracking pixels or first-party analytics.

Game progress and preferences, including your current garden, progress and sound setting, may be stored locally in your browser using local storage. This information remains on your device and is used so that a game can be restored when you return.

Underfoot game code does not intentionally transmit your saved game state to the operator.

You can remove locally stored game information using your browser's site-data or storage controls. Clearing browser storage may reset your saved garden and preferences.
