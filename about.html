About

A small puzzle about making room to grow.

Underfoot is a simple browser puzzle in which a field mouse moves through a sunflower garden. Each step plants a new shoot, and groups of three matching plants grow into the next stage.

How it works

The mouse can move only onto neighbouring bare soil. Each landing leaves a new shoot behind. Three matching plants in a row, column or diagonal grow together, progressing from shoot to bud, sunflower and finally seed head.

When more than one group of three is possible, you choose which group grows. The puzzle is therefore as much about preserving space and movement as it is about making matches.

Plan Keep enough open ground for the mouse to continue.

Design

Underfoot was designed around a small set of rules that are easy to learn but interact in increasingly difficult ways. Growth is rewarding, but every plant also occupies space. A successful move can therefore solve one problem while creating another.

The game is deliberately compact and quiet. There are no accounts, currencies, levels or daily rewards. A garden, a mouse and a few rules are enough.
